Projection desk

    How the map works. Before results come in, each county is shaded by a uniform-swing model: the county's 2024 presidential margin, shifted by the difference between my statewide projection and the 2024 statewide result (R+13.7). Once official county returns come in (automatically, from the Texas Secretary of State), counties are shaded by actual votes; counties still awaiting returns are shown faded.

    Other signals. Polling averages, market odds and official returns update automatically and are shown separately; they never change my projection. The polling average uses only trusted pollsters (Texas Politics Project, UT Tyler, UH Hobby School, Texas Tribune partners, Emerson, TPOR, Quinnipiac, Marist, NYT/Siena), weighted by recency (14-day half-life) and sample size, with registered-voter polls nudged toward likely-voter results. Market odds come from Kalshi and Polymarket. Official county returns come from the Texas Secretary of State's election-night results site.

    Turnout. Early-vote totals (in person and by mail) come from the Texas Secretary of State's daily early-voting reports, which county election officials submit; Election-night ballots cast come from the SOS results site. Turnout is shown as a share of registered voters. The comparison curves are the 2022 midterm and 2024 presidential early votes on the same number of days before Election Day.

    Projections on this site are my own and are not official results. Official results: Texas Secretary of State. County boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau via us-atlas.
