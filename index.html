    How the map works. Before results come in, each county is shaded by a uniform-swing model: the county's 2024 presidential margin, shifted by the difference between my statewide projection and the 2024 statewide result (R+13.7). Once county returns are entered, counties are shaded by actual votes; counties still awaiting returns are shown faded.

    Projections on this site are my own and are not official results. Official results: Texas Secretary of State. County boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau via us-atlas.
